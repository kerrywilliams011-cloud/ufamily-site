NOV24
Feeding families · Thanksgiving week

Friendsgiving Dinner: a free Thanksgiving meal for Tacoma families

  • Tuesday, November 24, 2026 · 4–8 PM
  • Manuscript · 203 Tacoma Ave S, Tacoma
  • Free · no reservations · take-out available
Read more

This Thanksgiving week, UFamily.org and our community partners are opening the doors at Manuscript for a free Friendsgiving Dinner. It's first come, first served: no charges, no reservations, no questions asked, and take-out is available for families who need it.

Made possible by generous volunteers and donations from Get To Know EM (GTKE), The Tacoma Vegans, Manuscript, Trini Plate, Feed Forward Kids Camp, Tag Team Media, Krownless Kids, Kings Burgers and Epilogue Kitchen Collective (The Footnote Project). Bring your family, bring a friend, and share the word.

DEC4
Youth empowerment · Violence prevention

First Friday: The Next Gen live music showcase

  • Friday, December 4, 2026 · 7–10 PM
  • Edison Square · 5415 South Tacoma Way, Tacoma
  • Free admission · all ages
Read more

The Next Gen is a free, all-ages concert lifting up young local talent, built around youth empowerment and violence prevention: giving the next generation a stage, a positive night out and a community that shows up for them.

Hosted by Talk Wit Zi, with food by Radnor's, a full bar, raffle prizes and more. Presented with Edison Live, Krownless Kings, Krownless Kids, Get To Know EM (GTKE), Edison Square and UFamily.org.
